This is a camp test, but not the kind with a right answer. Each scene puts you somewhere slightly out of reach, a cracked wall, a long road, a room that will not decide, and offers two ways to move. You can only keep one. The road you leave behind stays with you as an echo.

Somewhere nearby, another you chose the other way.

Answer the way you would actually act, not the way you would like to be remembered. Nobody is keeping score where you can see it.

Before you begin, you’ll give your name and the name of a muse, someone who has shaped how you see things. You’ll also add a photo of them. At the end, your camp is revealed and a certificate is made for you to keep.

The scenes are written in Bahasa Indonesia. Your progress and result are saved on this device only.
